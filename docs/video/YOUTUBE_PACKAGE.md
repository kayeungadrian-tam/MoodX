## Description

```text
What if an online meeting did not need another engagement score—just a safer
opening for more people to think and contribute?

MoodX is exploring a facilitator-controlled participation layer for online
meetings. A playful cue creates a transition, Quiet Think protects 45 seconds
for reflection, and one explicit question helps the team surface a risk,
question, or alternative before committing.

This two-minute concept story shows the product hypothesis: fun opens the door,
structured voluntary participation passes through it, and value exists only
when the input improves understanding, a decision, or a follow-up.

MoodX does not score individuals, infer emotion, or treat silence as a measure
of engagement. The facilitator remains in control.
```
