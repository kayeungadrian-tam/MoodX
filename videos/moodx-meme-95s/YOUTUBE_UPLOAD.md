## Description

Everyone joins the meeting. The same voices speak. Useful ideas arrive five
minutes after the decision.

MoodX is a native macOS meeting mixer that combines the facilitator's
microphone with on-demand sound effects and routes the result locally through
BlackHole into Microsoft Teams. It also includes a meeting timer, a protected
decision checkpoint, and a facilitator-controlled 45-second Quiet Think.

The product hypothesis is simple: fun can interrupt a stale meeting rhythm and
create a lower-risk opening for participation. The value follows only when
that opening helps the team surface a useful question, risk, alternative, or
decision.

MoodX does not score individuals, infer emotions, or automatically decide when
to intervene. The facilitator remains in control.

**Fun is a doorway to participation, and value follows.**

AI disclosure: This concept video includes two AI-generated illustrations and
an AI-generated ElevenLabs narration supplied by the project owner. Meme cards,
motion design, the ambient music bed, captions, and the final edit were created
locally and human-reviewed. Product footage uses real MoodX screenshots. The
participation outcome shown is a product hypothesis, not a validated claim.
